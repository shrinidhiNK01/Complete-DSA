No contiguous location in heap
Used in stack and Queue, and browser
DLL used in Browser
It is a Dynamic Data Structure, Grow and Shrink on its own
Scalable data Structure, Used in Building Graph and Trees

Types of Linked List:
=====================
1)Singly
2)Doubly
3)Circular

Time Complexity:-
================
Insertion at Head:-O(1)
Insertion at End:-O(n)
Insertion at Middle:-O(n)

Deletion at Head:-O(1)
Deletion at End:-O(n)
Deletion at Middle:-O(n)

Searching:-O(n)
Iteration:-O(n)

To find Cycle in Singly Linked List:- 
Floyd's Tortoise and Hare Algorithm

To find Middle of Linked List:- 
Floyd's Tortoise and Hare Algorithm

To find Length of Linked List:- 
Simple Iteration

Reverse Linked List:- 
1)Iterative
2)Recursive

To find Cycle in LL:- Fast and Slow Pointer and Middle Pointer
